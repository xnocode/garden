## Properties of Conjugate Complex Numbers

### Questions

#### i) Conjugate of a Sum

# $$  
\overline{z_1+z_2}

\overline{z_1}+\overline{z_2}  
$$

#### ii) Conjugate of a Difference

# $$  
\overline{z_1-z_2}

\overline{z_1}-\overline{z_2}  
$$

#### iii) Conjugate of a Product

# $$  
\overline{z_1z_2}

\overline{z_1}\cdot\overline{z_2}  
$$

#### iv) Conjugate of a Quotient

# $$  
\overline{\left(\frac{z_1}{z_2}\right)}

\frac{\overline{z_1}}{\overline{z_2}}  
$$

#### v) Sum of a Complex Number and Its Conjugate

# $$  
z_1+\overline{z_1}

2\operatorname{Re}(z_1)  
$$

#### vi) Product of a Complex Number and Its Conjugate

# $$  
z\cdot\overline{z}

# |\overline{z}|^2

|z|^2  
$$

#### vii) Conjugate of a Conjugate

$$  
\overline{\overline{z}}=z  
$$

#### viii) Difference Between a Complex Number and Its Conjugate

# $$  
z-\overline{z}

2\operatorname{Im}(\overline{z})  
$$

#### ix) Modulus of a Complex Number and Its Conjugate

$$  
|\overline{z}|=|z|  
$$

#### x) Relation Between Modulus and Real Part

$$  
|z|\geq \operatorname{Re}(z)  
$$

> **Note:** I have kept the formulas according to the handwritten page. The formula in **(viii)** appears to be missing an $i$ in the source; I have not silently changed it.
