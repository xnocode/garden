# Quantum Field Theory — Course Notes
These handwritten derivations were recorded using an iPad and physical paper scans.

## Page 1
$$ (i\gamma^\mu \partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0 $$
The **Dirac equation** describes relativistic spin-1/2 fermions (electrons, positrons, quarks) unifying special relativity and quantum mechanics.

## Page 2
$$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{QED}} = \bar{\psi}(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu - m)\psi - \frac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu} $$
Quantum Electrodynamics Lagrangian invariant under local $U(1)$ gauge transformations with covariant derivative $D_\mu = \partial_\mu + ieA_\mu$.

## Page 3
$$ \mathcal{Z}[J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi \, \exp\left(i \int d^4x \, (\mathcal{L} + J\phi)\right) $$
**Feynman Path Integral** formulation generating Green's functions and quantum amplitudes across all spacetime trajectories.

## Page 4
$$ \beta(g) = \mu \frac{\partial g}{\partial \mu} = -\frac{11 C_A - 4 n_f T_F}{12\pi} g^3 $$
Renormalization group beta function establishing asymptotic freedom and running coupling constants at high energy limits.
